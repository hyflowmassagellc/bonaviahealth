Frequently asked questions

Do I need a visa?

Since 20 August 2026, citizens of Kyrgyzstan can use China's 240-hour (10-day) visa-free transit when travelling on to a third country, and can enter Hainan province visa-free for up to 30 days. For treatment elsewhere in China or for longer stays, a visa is usually needed — we check the current rules for your case and help with the documents.

Will I be able to talk to the doctors?

Yes. A medical interpreter accompanies you to consultations, tests and procedures.

How much will treatment cost?

It depends on the diagnosis and hospital. Before you travel, we get an itemized estimate from the hospital and explain it to you.

Can a relative come with me?

Yes. We can help arrange travel and accommodation for an accompanying family member.

Sources: National Immigration Administration of China (Aug 2026) · Daryo — visa access for Central Asia (Aug 2026) · Guangdong — 25 international medical service pilot hospitals
