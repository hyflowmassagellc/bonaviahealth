Clear Roles

What we do — and what we don't.

Bonavia Health is a non-clinical coordination service. Here is exactly where our role begins and ends.

What we do

  • ✓Compare options for you: international departments of public hospitals, private hospitals and specialist centers
  • ✓Review your records and send your case to suitable hospitals
  • ✓Get itemized cost estimates and timelines before you travel
  • ✓Book appointments, arrange visa letters, transfers and hotels
  • ✓Accompany you with a medical interpreter
  • ✓Stay in touch during treatment and after you return home

What we don't do

  • ✕We do not diagnose or treat — licensed doctors at partner hospitals do
  • ✕We do not prescribe medicines or make medical decisions for you
  • ✕We do not guarantee treatment results
  • ✕We do not hide fees — our coordination fee is agreed in writing before work begins

Why China

Major Chinese cities are well-connected to Central Asia, Africa, and beyond — especially convenient for our core markets, and workable for patients from anywhere.

省

Lower Cost

Modern hospitals and equipment, often at meaningfully lower cost than equivalent care in Europe or North America. Savings vary by condition, hospital, and treatment plan — your coordinator will walk you through a real, itemized estimate for your specific case.

合

Integrative Care

Many partner hospitals combine Western medicine with Traditional Chinese Medicine — including acupuncture, cupping, tuina therapeutic massage, and herbal medicine — as part of treatment and recovery, when appropriate for your condition.

Oncology & Rare Diseases

New treatments in Hainan — without a long wait.

The Boao Lecheng International Medical Tourism Pilot Zone in Hainan is China's special medical zone. Hospitals there can use medicines and medical devices already approved abroad but not yet available elsewhere in China — an option worth considering for patients with cancer or rare diseases.

FAQ

Questions you might have.

Does Bonavia Health provide diagnosis or treatment directly?

No. We are a care coordination service — we connect you with licensed hospitals and specialists, prepare documentation, and arrange logistics. All diagnosis and treatment is provided by licensed doctors at our partner hospitals.

Are cost estimates accurate? Will costs increase after I arrive?

We provide an itemized estimate before you travel. Actual cost depends on your condition, test results, and chosen hospital — your coordinator will flag any changes in advance so there are no surprises on arrival.

What if I don't speak Chinese?

We provide interpreter support in English, Russian, and French, accompanying you to appointments, tests, and follow-up conversations.

How is this different from contacting a hospital myself?

Going directly often means language barriers, unfamiliar processes, and no way to judge which hospital truly fits your condition. We handle case review, hospital matching, documentation, and interpretation — so you don't have to figure it out alone.

I'm not in Central Asia or Africa — can I still use this service?

Yes. Central Asia, Russian-speaking communities, and Africa are our core markets, but we also welcome inquiries from Europe, English-speaking countries, and elsewhere.
