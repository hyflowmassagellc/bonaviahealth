FAQ

Questions you might have.

Does Bonavia Health provide diagnosis or treatment directly?

No. We are a care coordination service — we connect you with licensed hospitals and specialists, prepare documentation, and arrange logistics. All diagnosis and treatment is provided by licensed doctors at our partner hospitals.

Are cost estimates accurate? Will costs increase after I arrive?

We provide an itemized estimate before you travel. Actual cost depends on your condition, test results, and chosen hospital — your coordinator will flag any changes in advance so there are no surprises on arrival.

What if I don't speak Chinese?

We provide interpreter support in English, Russian, and French, accompanying you to appointments, tests, and follow-up conversations.

How is this different from contacting a hospital myself?

Going directly often means language barriers, unfamiliar processes, and no way to judge which hospital truly fits your condition. We handle case review, hospital matching, documentation, and interpretation — so you don't have to figure it out alone.

I'm not in Central Asia or Africa — can I still use this service?

Yes. Central Asia, Russian-speaking communities, and Africa are our core markets, but we also welcome inquiries from Europe, English-speaking countries, and elsewhere.
