Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Your health information is personal. This policy explains what we collect when you contact Bonavia Health, why we need it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Bonavia Health (康程跨境医疗服务有限责任公司) is a medical care coordination service based in China. We help international patients find suitable hospitals and specialists, prepare cost estimates and arrange travel and interpretation. We do not diagnose or treat patients; all medical care is provided by licensed partner hospitals.

For any privacy question, contact us at info@bonaviahealth.org.

2. Information we collect

  • Inquiry form: your name, email, phone number, preferred language, and the description of your situation. This description may include health information.
  • Documents you send us later by email, WhatsApp, WeChat or Telegram: medical records, test results, imaging, and — if you decide to travel — passport and travel details needed for visa letters and bookings.
  • Basic technical data, such as your IP address, processed by our website hosting and form providers to deliver the site and filter spam.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking tools on this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry and understand your needs.
  • To review your case and request treatment options and cost estimates from partner hospitals.
  • To arrange appointments, visa invitation letters, travel, accommodation and interpreters.
  • To stay in touch with you before, during and after your treatment.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We never sell your information, and we do not use your health information for marketing.

4. Your consent

By submitting our form or sending us documents, you agree that we may process your information — including health information — for the purposes above, and share it with partner hospitals as described below. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us; this does not affect processing that took place before.

5. Who we share it with

  • Partner hospitals and doctors in China — only the information needed to assess your case, and only after you ask us to proceed.
  • Interpreters and travel providers involved in your trip — only what they need to help you.
  • Service providers that run our website and communications: GitHub Pages (website hosting), Web3Forms (form delivery), Zoho Mail (email), and the messaging apps you choose to use with us (WhatsApp, WeChat, Telegram).
  • Authorities, when we are required to by law.

6. International transfers

Our team and partner hospitals are in China, so your information will be transferred to and stored in China. Some of our service providers may process data in other countries, such as the United States. We share only what is necessary and choose established providers that protect data.

7. How long we keep it

If your inquiry does not lead to treatment, we delete it within 24 months. If we coordinate your care, we keep your file for as long as needed for your treatment and follow-up, and for any period required by law. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, receive a copy of it, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent. Email info@bonaviahealth.org and we will reply within 30 days. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information and limit access to the people who need it. Please remember that email and messaging apps are not completely secure; send only what we ask for, and tell us if you would prefer another way to share sensitive documents.

10. Patients under 18

If the patient is under 18, a parent or legal guardian should contact us and give consent on their behalf.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
